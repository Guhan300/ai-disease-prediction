# Educational Symptom Reference Notes

These notes are for educational RAG context only. They are not clinical guidelines
and must not be treated as personalized medical advice.

## Fever

Fever is a temporary rise in body temperature often linked to infection or inflammation.
Common associated symptoms can include chills, sweating, headache, and fatigue.
Seek urgent care for very high fever with confusion, stiff neck, severe breathing
difficulty, or persistent vomiting.

## Cough and Cold

A common cold often involves runny nose, sore throat, mild cough, and sneezing.
Influenza more often includes higher fever, body aches, and marked fatigue.
Persistent cough with breathlessness or chest pain needs professional evaluation.

## Breathing Difficulty

Shortness of breath can occur with respiratory infections, asthma flares, and other
conditions. Sudden severe breathing trouble, blue lips, or inability to speak full
sentences may require emergency care.

## Gastrointestinal Symptoms

Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and abdominal pain can occur with gastroenteritis and
other illnesses. Watch for dehydration, blood in stool, severe pain, or high fever.

## Headache and Migraine

Headaches have many causes. Migraine may include nausea and light sensitivity.
Sudden worst-ever headache, neurological weakness, or confusion needs urgent care.

## Skin Rash and Itching

Rashes may relate to allergy, viral illness, or other dermatologic conditions.
Difficulty breathing with rash/swelling can signal a severe allergic reaction.

## Important Limitation

Similar symptoms appear across many conditions. Only a qualified clinician can
diagnose and treat. Educational ML estimates are not diagnoses.
